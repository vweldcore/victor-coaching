About Victor

Victor Ugbesia brings a unique path to counseling — engineer, fabricator, and founder, before advisor. He trained at The Polytechnic, Ibadan, married at 27, and built two companies from the ground up — starting with a daycare centre, then founding VWELDCORE Innovations Nigeria Limited at 28.

For over 5 years, he has counseled men, parents, youth, and couples — drawing on lived experience, not theory. His mission is simple: nobody is meant to be looked down on, including by themselves. There is greatness in everyone, and his work is helping people see it in themselves.

Focus areas: Men's Issues · Parental & Child Care Guidance · Youth Counseling · Marital Counseling
